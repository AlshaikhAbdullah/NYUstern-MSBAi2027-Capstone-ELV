# The Global ELV Recycling Gap

> Quantifying, predicting, and prescribing solutions to the global end-of-life vehicle (ELV) recycling gap.

**NYU Stern School of Business — MSBA Capstone 2027**

A strategic research initiative applying advanced analytics to a growing and largely invisible global recycling challenge.

---

## The Problem

As global vehicle demand accelerates and the first wave of electric vehicles approaches end-of-life, the world faces a large and largely invisible recycling gap. Millions of vehicles are informally disposed of every year in developing markets — representing billions in lost recoverable material value and significant environmental harm.

A key insight driving this project: **vehicles end their lives where they are *used*, not where they are *produced*.** Developing economies absorb large volumes of new and used vehicles through trade, so their future ELV burden is larger than domestic production figures alone would suggest.

## Project Goals

The project follows a four-stage analytical arc — descriptive → predictive → causal → prescriptive.

1. **Descriptive** — Map and quantify the global ELV recycling gap across countries, with confidence-tiered estimates and full methodology transparency.
2. **Predictive** — Estimate and project ELV generation and material value at risk by region, with explicit uncertainty quantification.
3. **Causal** — Identify the policy, regulatory, and economic factors most associated with better recycling outcomes.
4. **Prescriptive** — Develop an interactive dashboard that quantifies the ROI of closing the gap for companies, ESG investors, and policymakers.

> **Scope note:** Goals 1 and 2 are the core deliverables. Goals 3 and 4 are stretch targets to be scoped as time allows.

## Data Sources

| Source | Dataset | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **OICA** | Annual Vehicle Production | Vehicle production by country/type since 1997 |
| **UN Comtrade** | HS Code 8703 | Used-vehicle import/export flows by country pair |
| **IRF** | International Road Federation | Total registered vehicle fleet by country |
| **World Bank** | What a Waste 2.0 | MSW generation, landfill volumes, composition by country |

Additional sources under evaluation include Eurostat ELV statistics, US EPA / BTS fleet data, IDIS, LME commodity prices, and UNEP waste-management benchmarks.
